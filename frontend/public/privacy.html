Privacy and cookies

Updated 19 October 2026

Jared Fixes is operated by Jared Talbot in Wexford, Ireland. For privacy questions or requests, email fix@jaredtalbot.com.

Your enquiries

When you contact us, we use the details you provide to reply, prepare a quote and provide the service you request. The contact form asks for your name, email and enquiry details, with optional phone and location details. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information through the form.

The website and form are hosted by Netlify. Messages are handled through our email service, Proton. These providers process the information needed to deliver their services. We use enquiry details to take steps at your request before a contract and to carry out agreed work. Where necessary, we also retain records to meet legal obligations or handle business queries and claims. We keep information only as long as needed for those purposes.

Optional website analytics

Google Analytics is disabled until you choose Accept analytics. If you accept, Google receives information about visits, pages, device/browser details, approximate location and interactions such as scrolling, link clicks and successful enquiries. We do not send your form answers, name, email, phone number or message to Analytics. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in our website configuration.

Analytics uses cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish visits. Google processes this information on its infrastructure, which may involve processing outside the European Economic Area under its applicable data transfer arrangements. Read how Google uses information from partner sites and Google's privacy policy.

We store your accept or reject choice on your device for up to 180 days. You can reopen Cookie settings in the website footer at any time to withdraw consent. Rejecting or withdrawing does not affect access to our services. Withdrawal stops future Analytics collection and removes Analytics cookies accessible to this website; it does not undo earlier processing.

Hosting and your rights

Our hosting provider processes technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and protect the website. You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal information, and object or request portability where applicable. Contact us at the email above. You may also complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission.
